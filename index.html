Our Story

Built for people who care about where they live.

Lumière was born from a simple belief — that great furniture should be honest about its materials, built to outlast trends, and priced without compromise to quality.

We work directly with master craftspeople across India to bring you pieces that tell a story. From A-grade teak jointed without shortcuts, to marine-grade powder-coated frames that survive coastal climates — every piece in our collection is something we stand behind unconditionally.

Our Mission

To make premium furniture accessible without sacrificing the quality or care that makes it worth owning.

Our Vision

A home where every piece has meaning — furniture that grows with families and improves with age.
